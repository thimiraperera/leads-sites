Getting started

What BlinQ is, who it’s for and how to try it.

What is BlinQ Software?

BlinQ is an all-in-one, cloud-based business management platform designed for the window furnishing industry. It helps businesses manage customer enquiries, appointments, measurements, quotations, jobs, suppliers, purchase orders, invoices and payments from one central system.

Who is BlinQ Software designed for?

BlinQ is built for blinds, curtains, shutters and awnings retailers, manufacturers, wholesalers, dealers and franchise businesses. Whether you operate a small showroom or manage multiple teams and locations, BlinQ can be configured to suit your workflow.

Can I try BlinQ Software before subscribing?

Yes! BlinQ offers a 7-day free trial so you can explore the software before committing. A valid payment card is required to activate your trial, but you won’t be charged during the trial period. Subscription billing begins after the trial unless you cancel before it ends.

Do I need technical knowledge to use BlinQ?

Not at all. BlinQ is designed for everyday business users, including sales representatives, estimators, office administrators and installers. Our team can also provide one-on-one demonstrations and training to help you get started.

Can I use BlinQ on my phone, tablet and computer?

Yes. BlinQ is cloud-based and can be accessed from compatible smartphones, tablets, laptops and desktop computers with an internet connection. Your team can work from the office, showroom or customer site.

Quoting & pricing

On-site quotes, supplier price lists, markups and products.

Can BlinQ generate quotations while I’m at a customer’s home?

Absolutely! Your sales representatives can enter measurements, select products, fabrics, colours and options, and generate professional quotations directly from the customer’s location. This reduces manual calculations and the need to return to the office to prepare quotes.

Can BlinQ calculate prices using my supplier’s price lists?

Yes. BlinQ can be configured with supplier price grids, product options, pricing rules, markups and discounts. Once configured, the software calculates prices using your business’s pricing structure, helping reduce manual errors.

Can I manage different markups and discounts for different products?

Yes. You can configure pricing and markup rules for different product groups and apply permitted adjustments when preparing quotations. This gives you control over your selling prices and profit margins.

Does BlinQ support different types of window furnishings?

Yes. BlinQ supports a wide range of products, including roller blinds, Roman blinds, Venetian blinds, vertical blinds, honeycomb blinds, curtains, shutters, awnings, outdoor blinds and more. Product configuration depends on your suppliers and account setup.

Can I convert an approved quotation into a job?

Yes. Once a quotation is approved, it can be progressed into your job management workflow without recreating the entire order. Your team can then manage the next steps, including ordering, production, installation and completion.

CRM, scheduling & jobs

Customers, appointments, calendars and job tracking.

Does BlinQ include a CRM to manage customers and enquiries?

Yes. BlinQ includes customer relationship management tools that help you organise enquiries, customer details, communication history, quotations and jobs in one place. Your team can access relevant customer information without searching through multiple systems.

Can I schedule appointments and assign them to staff?

Yes. BlinQ provides appointment scheduling and calendar management tools. You can organise sales consultations, measurements and installations, assign appointments to team members and keep your schedule organised.

Does BlinQ integrate with Google Calendar?

Yes. BlinQ supports Google Calendar integration, allowing your team to connect supported appointments and scheduling activities with their calendars.

Can I track the progress of customer jobs?

Yes. BlinQ’s job management dashboard helps you monitor jobs through different stages, such as approved, check measurement, on hold, ready to fit, installed and completed. Job statuses can be configured to match your business processes.

Can BlinQ send appointment reminders and customer follow-ups?

Yes. BlinQ provides email and SMS communication options to help businesses send reminders, updates and follow-ups. Automation options and SMS charges may vary depending on your setup.

Manufacturing, suppliers & eCommerce

Production, purchase orders, dealer portals and online sales.

Can manufacturers use BlinQ to manage production?

Yes. BlinQ offers manufacturing-focused features, including order management, production workflows, worksheets, labels and reporting. The available functionality depends on the manufacturing plan and configuration.

Can I create and manage supplier purchase orders?

Yes. BlinQ helps businesses generate purchase orders from job information, manage suppliers and organise purchasing activities. This reduces duplicate data entry and helps teams keep track of orders.

Can wholesalers provide their dealers with their own quoting portal?

Yes. BlinQ’s wholesale and dealer solutions allow businesses to provide dealers with access to configured products and pricing through dedicated portals. Branding, dealer pricing and access permissions can be tailored to the wholesale setup.

Can I sell blinds and curtains online using BlinQ?

Yes. BlinQ offers eCommerce solutions that allow customers to select window furnishing products, enter measurements, choose available options and obtain pricing online. Solutions can be integrated into an existing website or developed as part of a complete online store.

Can BlinQ connect with accounting and payment systems?

Yes. BlinQ supports integrations with accounting platforms such as Xero, QuickBooks and MYOB, along with payment solutions including Stripe. Integration availability and setup requirements depend on your selected plan and configuration.

Pricing, setup & support

Plans, users, contracts, onboarding and training.

How much does BlinQ Software cost?

BlinQ offers subscription plans based on your business type, required features and number of users. Retail plans currently start at A$450 per month for three users, while manufacturing and wholesale plans start at A$750 per month for ten users, based on our published Australian pricing. Visit our pricing page for current plans and applicable setup fees.

Is there a minimum number of users?

Yes. The minimum number of licences depends on your plan. Our standard Retail plan requires a minimum of three users, while Manufacturing and Wholesale plans have a minimum of ten users.

Do I need to sign a long-term contract?

BlinQ offers monthly subscription plans without a mandatory annual commitment for standard plans. A 30-day cancellation notice applies. Please review the applicable subscription terms for your account.

How long does it take to set up my BlinQ account?

Your account can be activated quickly, while the complete configuration depends on your suppliers, products, price lists and workflow requirements. Standard onboarding can often be completed within approximately 10 business days after all required information is supplied. More complex configurations may take longer.

Will BlinQ help me import my existing product and supplier pricing?

Yes. Our onboarding team can assist with configuring supplier information, products, fabrics, colours, pricing grids and business-specific rules. The scope of data entry and any applicable setup fees depend on your selected plan.

Does BlinQ provide training and ongoing support?

Yes. BlinQ provides onboarding assistance, one-on-one online training options and ongoing support. You can also watch our training videos on YouTube.

Can I upgrade my plan or add more users as my business grows?

Yes. BlinQ is designed to grow with your business. You can discuss additional licences, plan upgrades and extra features with our team as your operational requirements change.

Security, integrations & customisation

Data protection, API access and tailored workflows.

Is my business and customer data secure with BlinQ?

BlinQ uses security measures and access controls designed to protect business and customer information. Access to your account is managed through authorised users and permissions. For further details about data handling, please review our Privacy Policy or contact our support team.

Does BlinQ offer API access and custom integrations?

Yes. BlinQ offers API and custom integration options for businesses that need to connect other platforms, suppliers or external systems. API access, available data and integration requirements are reviewed based on your business needs and the applicable access permissions. See our API documentation.

Can BlinQ be customised to suit the way my business operates?

Yes. BlinQ provides configurable products, pricing rules, workflows, job statuses and user access options. For more specialised requirements, our team can assess custom development or integrations, with scope, cost and timeframes agreed before work begins.
