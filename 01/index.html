FAQ

Fair questions.
Clear answers.

Is BlinQ built for window furnishing businesses?+

Yes. BlinQ supports businesses working with blinds, curtains, shutters and awnings, with workflows for retailers, manufacturers, wholesalers and franchises.

Can we quote at the customer’s property?+

Yes. Create quotations on site using your configured product pricing and options, then email the quotation to your customer.

Can we use our own products and pricing?+

Yes. Product and pricing setup is discussed during onboarding so your quotation workflow reflects your business requirements.

Can BlinQ connect with our accounting software?+

BlinQ offers integrations with Xero, QuickBooks and MYOB. We’ll confirm the available integration and any setup requirements during your demo.

What happens after I submit the form?+

Your enquiry is saved, and you can choose a time using the BlinQ booking calendar. Submitting the form alone does not book a meeting.

How much does it cost?+

Pricing depends on your plan, users, modules and setup requirements. We’ll discuss the suitable options for your business. You can also view the current pricing on the BlinQ website.

Is there a free trial?+

BlinQ offers a seven-day free trial. A payment card is required for the trial, with billing after the trial under the applicable terms. No payment card is needed to request a demo.
